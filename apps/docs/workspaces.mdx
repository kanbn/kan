A workspace holds your boards, the people who work on them, and your [plan](/plans). Most teams need one. Create more to keep separate teams or clients apart; each has its own members and its own plan.

## Create and switch workspaces

Click the workspace name at the top of the sidebar to switch workspaces or create a new one.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Kan">
    Open the workspace menu in the sidebar and choose **Create workspace**.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="API">
    ```bash
    curl -X POST https://kan.bn/api/v1/workspaces \
      -H "Authorization: Bearer $KAN_API_KEY" -H "content-type: application/json" \
      -d '{"name": "Acme"}'
    ```
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Agent">
    "Create a workspace called Acme."
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Settings

Admins can change these in **Settings → Workspace**:

| Setting              | What it does                                                                                                                                            |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Name and description | How the workspace appears to members.                                                                                                                   |
| Workspace URL        | The name in your public board links, such as `kan.bn/acme/roadmap`. Between 3 and 64 letters, numbers and hyphens. Choosing your own needs a paid plan. |
| Email visibility     | Whether members can see each other's email addresses.                                                                                                   |
| Week start day       | Which day weeks start on in the calendar view and date pickers.                                                                                         |

## Search

Press `⌘K` (or `Ctrl+K`) to search the boards and cards in the current workspace and jump straight to one.

## Delete a workspace

Admins can delete a workspace from **Settings → Workspace**. This deletes its boards and cards for everyone and can't be undone. On a paid plan, cancel the subscription first from **Settings → Billing → Billing portal**.
