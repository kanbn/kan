## 1. Sign up

Go to [kan.bn/signup](https://kan.bn/signup) and sign up with your email or Google. You'll be asked to name your first workspace and pick a plan. Free is fine to start with; you can [upgrade](/plans) any time.

## 2. Create a board

On [Boards](https://kan.bn/boards), click **New** and give the board a name. Turn on **Use template** to start from a ready-made set of lists and labels, such as Basic Kanban (To Do, In Progress, Done).

Already on Trello? Click **Import** instead. See [Import from Trello](/integrations/trello).

## 3. Add cards

Click **+** at the top of a list, type a title and press enter. Click a card to open it and add a description, labels, a due date, assignees or a checklist. Drag cards between lists as work moves along.

Prefer code? The [Boards](/boards#create-a-board) and [Cards](/cards#create-and-edit-cards) guides show each step with the API.

## 4. Invite your team

Inviting people needs the [Team plan](/plans). Open **Members**, click **Invite**, and enter their email. They get an email with a link to join. Or create an invite link that anyone you send it to can use.

New members join as a **member**: they see the boards you add them to. Make someone an **admin** to let them see every board and manage the workspace. See [Members and roles](/members).
