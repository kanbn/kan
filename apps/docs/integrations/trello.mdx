---

Import brings your Trello boards across as Kan boards. You can import as many boards as you like, on any plan.

## What's imported

| Trello     | Kan                                                  |
| ---------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| Board      | Board                                                |
| Open lists | Lists, in the same order                             |
| Open cards | Cards, with their title, description and due date    |
| Labels     | Labels. Unnamed labels are named after their colour. |
| Checklists | Checklists, with each item and whether it's done     |

Archived lists and cards, comments, attachments, members and Power-Ups aren't imported. Your Trello boards aren't changed.

## Import your boards

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Trello">
    Open **Settings → Integrations** and click **Connect Trello**. Log in to
    Trello and approve access. You're sent back to Kan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your boards">
    On **Boards**, click **Import**, choose **Trello** and click **Select
    source**. Pick the boards to import, or **Select all**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import">
    Click import. Boards appear in the current workspace as they finish. Big
    boards can take a minute.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each board is imported on its own, so if one fails the rest still come across. Kan tells you which didn't and why. See [Troubleshooting](/troubleshooting#a-trello-import-didnt-finish).

To remove Kan's access, click **Disconnect Trello** in **Settings → Integrations**.
