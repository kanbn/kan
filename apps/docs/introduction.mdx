Kan is a kanban board for you and your team. Plan work on boards, break it into cards, and move cards across lists as it gets done. Use it in the browser, from your AI agent over MCP, or from your own code with the REST API.

## How it fits together

There are four things to know about:

- **[Workspaces](/workspaces)** hold your team, your boards and your plan. You can belong to more than one.
- **[Boards](/boards)** are a project or process, split into **lists** such as To Do, In Progress and Done. Boards are private unless you make one public.
- **[Cards](/cards)** are the work itself. They have a description, labels, assignees, a due date, checklists, comments and attachments, and every change is recorded in the card's activity.
- **[Members](/members)** are the people in a workspace. Their role decides which boards they see and what they can change.

These docs cover [Kan Cloud](https://kan.bn). Kan is also open source, so you can [run it yourself](/self-hosting/introduction).
