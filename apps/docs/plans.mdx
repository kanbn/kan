Kan is free for one person. Upgrade a workspace to the Team plan to work on it with others. Plans are per workspace.

The Team plan starts with a 14-day free trial. Cancel any time before it ends and you won't be charged.

## Seats

On the Team plan, each member is a seat. Inviting someone adds a seat to your subscription, and removing them takes it off, so you only pay for the people in the workspace. Kan tells you the price before you invite someone.

## Upgrade, change or cancel

Admins can upgrade from **Settings → Billing → Choose plan**, or from **Members** when inviting someone. **Billing portal** opens Stripe, where you can change your card, see invoices or cancel.

## When a paid plan ends

If you cancel, or a payment fails and isn't retried successfully, the workspace moves to Free at the end of the billing period:

- Every member except the owner is [paused](/members#paused-members). Nothing is deleted.
- A custom workspace URL goes back to the default one, so public board links change.

Subscribe again and paused members are restored.

Self-hosted Kan has no plans or limits. See [Self-hosting](/self-hosting/introduction).
